# VELTRIXA website starter

## Included
- Responsive single-page website (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`)
- Your supplied logo in `assets/veltrixa-logo.jpeg`
- Basic SEO metadata, canonical URL, Open Graph metadata, Organization structured data
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml`

## Before publishing
1. Confirm and register your domain. The starter assumes `www.veltrixa.com`; domain availability has not been checked.
2. If you use a different domain (for example `.in`), replace every `https://www.veltrixa.com/` occurrence in `index.html`, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml`.
3. Business email is already configured as `team.veltrixa.ind@gmail.com` in the website and contact form.
4. Update the contact details/social links once you provide them. No phone number, address, or social handle has been invented.
5. Upload the site to Vercel, Netlify, or your preferred host. For Vercel, import the folder/repository and use the default static settings.
6. After the domain is connected and the site is live, add the domain property in Google Search Console, verify ownership, and submit `https://www.veltrixa.com/sitemap.xml` (or your actual domain's sitemap).

## Important SEO note
This site includes good technical SEO foundations, but no one can guarantee a #1 Google ranking. Google must crawl and index the live site, and rankings depend on relevance, competition, trust, links, content quality, and other factors. Keep your business name consistent across your website and public business profiles.
